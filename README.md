# Sevak Petrosyan

Co-Founder & CTO at [Gostex](https://gostex.io/), building payment infrastructure, AI-powered operations, and reliable production systems.

I stay hands-on across architecture, backend and product engineering, data pipelines, rollout, and production verification. I like systems whose failure modes are explicit, operator actions are auditable, and changes are safe to reverse.

## What I work on

- **Payment infrastructure:** provider integrations, routing and cascades, pay-ins and payouts, callbacks, idempotency, ledgers, and reconciliation.
- **Event-driven data:** Kafka, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, replay and backfills, deduplication, observability, and safe migrations.
- **AI-powered operations:** typed tools and agents with dry runs, redaction, audit trails, credential safety, and least-privilege access.
- **Product systems:** PHP/Laravel, TypeScript/React, Python/FastAPI, APIs, admin tools, and mobile-web flows.

## How I work

- Take ambiguous production problems from diagnosis to verified delivery.
- Design for data correctness, observability, rollback, and operational safety.
- Test failure paths and real end-to-end behavior, not only the happy path.
- Keep architecture close to product outcomes and operator reality.
